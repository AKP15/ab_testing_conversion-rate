###Caculate Conversion Rate 
Conversion Rate = Converted Users ÷ Total Users × 100

###Caculate Conversion Lift
Conversion Lift = (B Conversion Rate − A Conversion Rate) / A Conversion Rate × 100
<Users who saw Version B converted at a rate approximately 22% higher than users who saw Version A.>

###Two-Proportion Z-Test
pA = xA / NA  conversion/number of user                     
pB = xB / NB
Calculate Pooled Proportion                                      
p̂ = (xA + xB) / (NA + NB)
SE = √[p̂(1 − p̂)(1/NA + 1/NB)]
z-score = (pB − pA) / SE
p-value = 2 × P(Z > |z|)

###CI 
diff = p_b - p_a
SE unpool= √[pA(1−pA)/n_A + pB(1−pB)/n_B]
lower = diff - 1.96 * se_unpooled
upper = diff + 1.96 * se_unpooled
Margin of error = 1.96 × SE unpool
<+1.09 and +3.42 percentage points
with 95% confidence.>
